# DBS Bank - Risk Management Analysis >

A case study of a portfolio which takes a university risk management assignment and turns it into an interactive dashboard for recruiters.

## Abstract

This project examines DBS Bank’s enterprise risk landscape through three industry-standard frameworks — **ISO 31000:2018**, **COSO ERM 2017**, and **DBS’s own Risk Appetite Framework** — across eight identified risks in four categories.

The analysis was done as an individual coursework for Risk Management module (FIN61904) at Taylor's University. Due to the depth of the research and the relevance of the topic, I transformed it into a structured case study to showcase applied analytical thinking in a portfolio context. ## What's Inside
- **Framework synthesis** — cross-mapping of three risk frameworks to assess DBS’ governance posture
- Risk heat map - 5x5 likelihood x impact matrix with eight risks plotted by category
- **KRI dashboard** - Five Key Risk Indicators measured against thresholds with breach flags
- **Trend analysis** — a directional chart of cyber incidents, system uptime and NPL ratio over time- **Mitigation playbook** — control recommendations by risk category ## Frameworks utilised | Framework | Purpose | |---|---| | ISO 31000:2018 | Principles for risk identification and treatment | | COSO ERM 2017 | Enterprise-wide governance framework | | DBS Risk Appetite Framework | Institution-specific tolerance thresholds |

## Covered Risk Categories

- Operational risk (cybersecurity, technology resiliency, third party vendors)- Credit risk (non-performing loans, climate-related lending)
- Market risk (interest rate exposure, foreign exchange volatility)- Strategic Risk (digital transformation execution) ## Technology Built as one self-contained HTML file with:
- CSS variables for light/dark theme support- SVG trend chart (no external chart library)
- Responsive layout for desktop and mobile

### Data Note

The KRI numbers are estimated by the author and are directionally informed by DBS’ public annual reports and Pillar 3 disclosures. They are not internal DBS data
